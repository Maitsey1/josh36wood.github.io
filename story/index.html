The full story

From a dirt bike to the grid

An American rider based in Málaga, coached by Nico Ferreira at SPN Academy. Four seasons from his first dirt bike to the ESBK Talent Cup as his target.

2022Starting out

First dirt bike, then the circuit

Josh gets his first dirt bike in 2022. He wants more speed than off-road can give him, so he moves to the circuit and starts training on a 165cc.

2023First races

The Spanish national cups

A few months after starting circuit training, Josh enters his first races in the Spanish national cups.

2024SPN Academy

Coached by Nico Ferreira

In 2024 Josh joins SPN Academy and trains under coach Nico Ferreira. His first season with Nico brings three steps up.

A wildcard ride with Quaresma

Josh is invited to race as a wildcard for Team Quaresma.

Wildcard in MIR Moto5

He races in the MIR Moto5 championship as a wildcard, against the series regulars.

Red Bull MotoGP Rookies Cup Selection Event

He is one of around 120 riders invited to the Selection Event, the entry route to the Red Bull MotoGP Rookies Cup.

2025PreMoto3

Stepping up to PreMoto3

In 2025 Josh moves up from Moto5 to PreMoto3, the next class on the path towards Moto3.

2026Full time

School finished, training full time

Josh finishes school in 2026 and trains full time. The week is built around riding: SPN Academy sessions and track days on his R6 at larger circuits, with strength, endurance and flat track work in between.

NextThe target

The ESBK Talent Cup

The next step is the ESBK Talent Cup. This is the programme partners back.
