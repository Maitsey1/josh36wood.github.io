Where he has raced

Josh trains with SPN Academy under coach Nico Ferreira. In 2024 he was one of about 120 riders at the Red Bull MotoGP Rookies Cup Selection Event. He raced the MIR Racing Cup in Moto5 and PreMoto3, and finished school in 2026 to commit to racing full time.

The next target is the ESBK Talent Cup. Partners are backing that step.
