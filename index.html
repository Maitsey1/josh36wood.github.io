The Rider

Built for
This

Josh Wood is 17 years old, American, and based in Malaga, Spain. He has one goal: to race at the highest level of motorcycle sport. That kind of focus at that age is rare — and it shows on the track.


He trains full-time at SPN Academy under coach Nico Ferreira, one of the most respected development environments in junior motorcycle racing. The work is daily — on the bike, in the gym, refining the margins that separate competitive riders from the rest. He is one of 120 riders worldwide selected for the Red Bull Rookies Cup tryouts, one of the most demanding talent identification processes in global motorsport.


In 2026 he competes in the European Talent Cup — the FIM's official road to MotoGP. Seven races across Europe, broadcast live across 14 international platforms.
