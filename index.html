About Josh

Josh Wood has finished school and now races full-time. He trains with SPN Academy under coach Nico Ferreira and competes across Europe. In 2024 he was one of around 120 riders invited to the Red Bull MotoGP Rookies Cup Selection Event. His objective for 2027 is the ESBK Talent Cup.

Race weekends are the short part

Track days at SPN Academy and on his R6 at larger circuits are what people see. The rest of the week is gym and strength work, long rides on the road bike, running, flat track and Hyrox-style conditioning.
